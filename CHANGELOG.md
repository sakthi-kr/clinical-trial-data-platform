# Changelog

## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-16

Initial tagged portfolio release of the synthetic study data platform. Includes reproducible study generation, manifest verification, typed transactional DuckDB ingestion, 19 configurable quality checks, a controlled fault-injection benchmark, a local Streamlit dashboard, review-event history and simplified synthetic study-domain exports.

Adds a clean-room verification command and documents the limits of the benchmark and regulatory claims. All examples use fictional data.
